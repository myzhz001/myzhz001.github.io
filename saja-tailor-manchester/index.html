Since 2009, Saja Tailor has been the go-to alterations shop in Manchester's Stonegate Shopping Center — not through advertising, but through consistent, precise work that earns repeat clients. Wedding gowns adjusted to perfection. Suits taken in with a sharp eye for proportion. Bridesmaid dresses hemmed to exactly the right length. "It was the perfect length," one client wrote after her bridesmaid dress alteration. That is the standard here.

Speed without cutting corners is a Saja hallmark. When one client dropped off five suit jackets without an appointment, every one came back done right and on schedule. "Nice, professional and prompt" — those three words appear in review after review. Whether it is a simple hem or a complex bridal gown, the turnaround is reliable and the work is clean.

Located in the heart of Manchester, Saja Tailor is open Monday through Saturday — convenient for West County residents in Ballwin, Chesterfield, and the surrounding area. Walk-ins are welcome. Bring your garments, describe what you need, and trust that the work will be done right.
