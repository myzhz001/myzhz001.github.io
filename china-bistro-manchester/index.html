Our Story

A Local Favorite in Manchester

China Bistro is the kind of place that loyal customers keep to themselves — tucked inside Pan Asia Supermarket on Manchester Rd, it serves some of the most authentic Chinese and Vietnamese food in St. Louis at prices that feel like 2005. The kitchen has been quietly feeding Manchester for a decade.

The menu spans two cuisines with equal mastery: traditional Chinese stir-fries and dim sum prepared the old-fashioned way, alongside Vietnamese pho with broth that simmers for hours. "The traditional, delicious Asian cuisine reminds you of home," regulars say — because it's made by people who grew up eating this food.

"This is easily the best for its combination of quality and price" — that's the verdict from customers who've traveled across St. Louis to compare. China Bistro doesn't need a flashy storefront or an online marketing budget. In Manchester, word of mouth has kept the tables full for ten years.
