Crystal Clear STL was started in 2018 by Angela Park, a South City homeowner who couldn't find a window cleaner that showed up on time and left no streaks — so she trained herself, bought the right equipment, and started taking neighbors' calls.

Today we serve over 600 homes and 80 businesses across St. Louis City and County. Our team of three keeps routes tight so we can offer same-week scheduling most of the year.

We're fully insured and we stand behind every clean. If you spot a streak within 24 hours, we come back and re-do it at no charge.
