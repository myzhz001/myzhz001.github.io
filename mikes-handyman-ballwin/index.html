West County Born, Neighborhood Proud 扎根西县，服务邻里 Nacido en West County, orgulloso de servir al vecindario

Mike started this business in 2009 after 10 years doing commercial maintenance for apartment complexes around St. Louis County. He saw how many homeowners struggled to find a reliable handyman for small jobs — the big contractors wouldn't bother, and the solo guys would disappear.

Today, Mike and his small crew handle everything from drywall patches to deck repairs, door installations to tile replacement. Most of his work comes from Nextdoor and word of mouth. That's not an accident — he shows up on time, gives honest quotes, and cleans up when he's done.

No job is too small. If you've been putting off that repair because you couldn't find the right person, call Mike. He's been fixing West County homes for 15 years and counting.
